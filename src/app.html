Score request

The two paths that matter in production: a bank asking for a score, and the platform telling the bank when a label changes.

Score a new loan site

Synchronous when the site is already observed, a job when it is new.

  1. 1Bank credit system→toAPI gatewayPOST /v1/sites:score with the site polygon, commodity and loan reference, an OAuth token and an idempotency key.
  2. 2API gateway→toScoring APIChecks scopes and quota, adds the tenant ID.
  3. 3Scoring API→toPostGISSaves the site and looks for observations newer than the freshness window.
  4. 4aScoring API→toSignal engineAlready observed: scores with the current methodology version and scenario. Target under 300 ms at the 95th percentile.
  5. 4bScoring API→toEvent busNew site: publishes site.created; the orchestrator runs zonal statistics (minutes). The API returns 202 with a job ID, then a webhook when the score is ready.
  6. 5Scoring API→toAudit logWrites the request, a hash of the inputs, the methodology version and the result.
  7. 6Scoring API→toBank credit systemReturns label, score, sub-scores, confidence and links to each piece of evidence.

Refresh data and alert on change

Runs when a source publishes new data.

  1. 1OrchestratorDetects a new source release, such as a yearly forest-loss version or new satellite scenes.
  2. 2Raster workers→toPostGISRecompute observations only for sites whose tiles changed, using a spatial index.
  3. 3Signal engineRe-scores those sites and compares each label with the last one.
  4. 4Event bus→toWebhookslabel.changed goes to the bank's system and shows as an alert in the web app.
  5. 5AI notes service→toAnalystDrafts an updated credit note; the eval gate checks every number against the evidence before an analyst sees it.

Methodology v0.1 (my sketch)

Score = weighted mean of sub-scores, 0–100, higher is healthier: forest 30% (Hansen loss 2021–2024), vegetation 20% (NDVI trend), water 20% (Aqueduct), biodiversity 20% (protected-area distance and threatened species), livelihoods 10%.

Nature Risk if score < 45, or ≥ 2% of the area lost tree cover in 2021–2024 (0.5% under the stricter rule). Nature Gain if score ≥ 62, vegetation improving and forest loss < 0.5%. Otherwise Nature Stable.

Confidence = 60% supply-chain traceability + 40% cloud-free satellite coverage.
