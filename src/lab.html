Experiment G · all 27 farm loans · scenarios

What each scenario does to the book

Real forest loss (Hansen/UMD v1.12), rainfall deficit (ERA5), vegetation change (Sentinel-2 NDVI), water stress (WRI Aqueduct) and distance to protected areas (WDPA), pulled from open data on 28 Oct 2026.

Sample loan sizes, borrower financials, crop sensitivities and bank pricing inputs. The capital formula is the Basel IRB corporate formula. The links from nature to cash flow are a v0.1 sketch for discussion, not Nature Signal's methodology, and would be calibrated on real loan outcomes.

Reproduce with python3 analysis/model_lab.py: it reads analysis/sites.json and writes analysis/results.json, which this page draws. The random seed is fixed at 20261110.
